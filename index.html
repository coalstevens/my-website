01 A little background

I'm a mechatronics engineer with experience designing biomedical instruments and custom automation equipment. My work has covered mechanical design, prototyping, and the hardware and software needed to get a system running.

Writing software is the part I enjoy most, whether it's controlling equipment, running a game, or making a website work. I'm currently pursuing a master's in computer science to deepen my knowledge of systems and AI.

I focus on creating systems that are comprehensive and intuitive. I believe the best solutions are the ones you don't notice, because they just work.
