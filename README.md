# Cole Stevens — My Website and Portfolio

[colestevens.ca](https://colestevens.ca)

My personal website, featuring my background in mechatronics and software, selected projects, and a workout log I built for my own training.

- **[Portfolio](https://colestevens.ca/)** — A little about me, my work and education, a downloadable résumé, and projects spanning automation, embedded systems, games, and web development.
- **[Workout log](https://colestevens.ca/workouts/)** — An interactive training log with editable exercises, session archives, Google sign-in, cloud sync, and CSV export. Try the public demo without an account.
- **[Training analysis](https://colestevens.ca/workouts/analysis.html)** — A closer look at training history, including workout frequency, exercise summaries, and performance trends.

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with Firebase for workout accounts and sync. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

---

<sub>“No tracking, just snacking” is a little white lie, at least if you take it to mean nothing gets stored. The site remembers your theme, and the workout app stores local copies of your workout history and keeps you signed in and synced with the database. Google sign-in may also use cookies. No analytics or advertising trackers, though. The snacking is real.</sub>
